    How it works

    Traditional NLP pipeline — transparent and explainable

    1. ExtractText is pulled from each PDF and DOCX in memory.
    2. NormalizeLowercasing, tokenization, stop-word removal and lemmatization.
    3. CompareTF-IDF vectors for the JD and all resumes; cosine similarity is computed.
    4. RankSimilarity (0–1) becomes a match % and candidates are ordered highest first.

    Scores measure textual similarity — word overlap between a resume and the posting — not probability of hiring success.
